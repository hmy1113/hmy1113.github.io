About the Lab

Biology becomes more useful when it can be measured reliably.

The Hee Min Yoo Lab investigates emerging biomedical platforms through the lens of measurement science. Our research spans organoid culture and analysis, extracellular vesicle measurement, organ-on-a-chip and microphysiological systems, molecular quantification, live-cell imaging, and standardization-oriented validation.

We aim to establish experimental workflows that are innovative yet quantitatively measurable, comparable across laboratories, and sufficiently robust for translational research and future standardization.

Our Approach
